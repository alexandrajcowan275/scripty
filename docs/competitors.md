# Scripty — competitive context

Primary-source review: **October 17, 2026**. These are adjacent products and categories, not a comprehensive market map. Capabilities below reflect the linked official pages at review time. The Scripty column states our chosen scope; it does not establish that another product lacks an equivalent feature.

| Product / category | Documented capability | Scripty's intended focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Runway — creative generation** | Gen-4 Image References uses reference images for characters, objects, and styles. Its guide describes consistent characters across lighting and locations, up to three references, saved tags, and iterative reuse of generated images. [Runway reference guide](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/40042718905875-Creating-with-Gen-4-Image-References). | A creator correction becomes persistent, testable memory that reaches later generation and monitoring. Reference-driven generation is complementary; Scripty must demonstrate value beyond providing a reference image. |
| **Frame.io — creative review** | Reviewers can leave single-frame, timecoded, and range-based comments on media, with annotations to make feedback specific. [Frame.io commenting guide](https://help.frame.io/en/articles/9105251-commenting-on-your-media). | Turn a continuity correction into a production constraint, then inspect its application in a different generated scene. The MVP centers one creator; team review and collaboration remain future work. |
| **Mem0 — persistent agent memory** | Mem0 describes memory that persists across sessions and agents, with extraction, updating, and retrieval of stored context. [Mem0 product page](https://mem0.ai/). | A domain-specific character sheet, visual check, creator decision, and applied-rule trace in a filmstrip workflow. Memory itself is an established category; Scripty's test is whether this production workflow helps creators. |
| **LangSmith — agent monitoring** | LangSmith provides tracing, cost tracking, online evaluations, and failure analysis across agent stacks. [LangSmith observability](https://www.langchain.com/langsmith/observability). | Inspect visual continuity against project character criteria and connect a flagged shot to a creator-approved rule. General observability and evaluations could support this product; its visual judgments still need validation. |

**Positioning hypothesis.** Scripty is the AI script supervisor for recurring-character production: a visible continuity problem becomes one remembered instruction, with evidence of where it went next. The narrow entry point is a small AI production team making repeated shots of the same character.

**What remains to prove.** That creators trust the checks, corrections survive and generalize to different scenes, and the workflow reduces review time or rerolls. The demo does not establish product uniqueness, market size, customer demand, pricing power, or savings. Monitor flags alone cannot establish better images. Validate those claims with human-reviewed comparisons, creator interviews, and paid pilots.
